01 / The person behind the code

A little about me.

My path into software engineering wasn't entirely linear. Before studying computer science, I trained as an industrial clerk and worked in marketing. Later, at the Philipps University of Marburg, I discovered a strong interest in graphics programming and interactive 3D applications.

Today, I work as a Senior 3D Graphics Engineer at Darmstadt Graphics Group, where I build and maintain tools that help artists and teams work with complex 3D data. I especially enjoy the space where software engineering meets graphics.

Outside of work, you'll often find me exploring game development, chess, indoor climbing, or electronics projects with a Raspberry Pi. I also like getting back to fundamentals through personal C++ rendering experiments.
